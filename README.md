Hello! I am a young artist who is going to be in 7th grade in the fall. I really enjoy making art and figured, why not share it with the world? I decided to make this little website so I can showcase and sell digital copies of my art. Me selling my art also has a cause other than just wanting a little pocket cash. In my upcoming seventh grade year, my class and the eighth grade plan on going to Washington DC. Since all the travel costs are going to be expensive, we need a little extra funding. Each student has to pay a decently hefty fee to go on this trip. So if you buy even just one piece of my art, not only are you getting some good art if I do say, but you are also contributing to me being able to go on an amazing school trip! And even if you dont buy anything, thanks for appreciating my art.
